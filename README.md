# SmartBudgetManager
A project for managing Budget
What it does
SmartBudget Manager is an Excel workbook that helps you record your monthly income and expenses, put expenses in categories, calculate your balance automatically, see a chart of expenses by category and receive a warning when you spend more than your budget.
What you need
    • Microsoft Excel 2016, 2019, 2021 or Microsoft 365 (or a compatible spreadsheet program).
    • The file SmartBudget_Manager.xlsx. No installation, no internet and no macros are needed.
What is inside
    • Dashboard: totals, balance, budget status, summary by category and chart.
    • Transactions: the place where you enter income and expenses.
    • Categories: the five categories and their monthly budgets.
Quick start
    1. Open SmartBudget_Manager.xlsx. If a yellow bar appears, click Enable Editing.
    2. Open the Categories worksheet and type the monthly budget of each category in column C.
    3. Open the Transactions worksheet and go to the first empty row.
    4. Enter the date and a description.
    5. Choose Income or Expense in the Type list. For an expense, choose a category in the Category list.
    6. Type the amount in the Income or Expense column and press Enter.
    7. Open the Dashboard to see the new totals, the balance and the budget status.
Good to know
    • The file contains sample data for September 2026. Delete cells A to G of rows 4 to 9 before you start your own month. Do not delete column H (Month).
    • Do not type in the Month column or on the Dashboard: they are calculated.
    • Only transaction rows 4 to 50 are counted.
    • Green WITHIN BUDGET means total expenses are lower than the total budget. Orange OVER BUDGET means they are higher.
    • Save a copy of the file before you start a new month (File > Save As).
